### Citation Standards
Priya raised inconsistencies in citation formatting across received chapters. The team agreed that all chapters must follow the Lantern Press house style for scientific citations: Author (Year) in-text, with full references in the chapter endnotes. Priya will send formatting guidelines to all contributors.

### Approved Manuscript Handling
Once a chapter passes final review and is marked as approved, it must not be modified without going through a formal revision request process. This protects against accidental changes during layout and also ensures our fact-checking record remains intact. Approved manuscripts will be clearly marked in their metadata.
