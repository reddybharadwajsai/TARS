# Editorial Policy — Lantern Press Coastal Ecology Collection

This policy governs the handling of documents within the Lantern Press editorial workspace. All team members, contributors, and automated tools must comply with these rules.

## Approved Manuscripts

Once a chapter or document has been marked as APPROVED (indicated by "APPROVED" in the document status or filename), it must NOT be modified, overwritten, moved, or deleted. Approved manuscripts have passed final editorial review and fact-checking. Any changes require a formal revision request submitted through the lead editor.

If edits are needed to an approved manuscript, create a new revision document (e.g., chapter_3_revision_notes.md) rather than modifying the original.

## Embargo Policy

Chapter 4 (Marine Conservation Policy) contains pre-publication data from a government review that is embargoed until March 1, 2025. Until the embargo lifts:

- Do NOT quote specific figures or data points from Chapter 4 in any summaries, briefings, or generated documents.
- Do NOT include Chapter 4 content in any public-facing materials.
- References to the general topic of marine conservation policy are permitted, but specific embargoed data must not be reproduced.

## Generated Content Requirements

Any document generated by automated tools or AI assistants MUST:

1. Include a list of source filenames consulted during preparation.
2. Be placed in the output/ folder unless explicitly directed otherwise by an editor.
3. Not present synthesized information as original claims without noting the source documents.

## Citation Requirements

Documents that reference or draw upon specific source documents must include citations following the Lantern Press citation format. When a source document's content is used, that document's filename must be noted in the generated output.

## File Organization

- Research notes belong in research_notes/
- Meeting records belong in meeting_records/
- Working drafts belong in drafts/
- Reference materials belong in reference/
- Style guides belong in style_guides/
- Generated output belongs in output/
- Do not create files outside the established folder structure without editor approval.
