## Overview

The coastal chapter is the most complex piece in the Lantern Press Coastal Ecology Collection. Recent editorial discussions have clarified its scope, organization, and production timeline.

## Chapter Scope

The coastal chapter addresses four interconnected sub-topics:
1. **Living Systems**: Coral reef ecosystems, tide pools, and mangrove forests
2. **Physical Processes**: Coastal erosion patterns, sedimentation, and sea level change

### Organization Decision

An initial proposal to split this content into two separate chapters was rejected due to word count allocation concerns and pricing model implications. Instead, the team decided to maintain it as a single chapter with **clear section breaks** that allow readers to navigate to their area of interest.
